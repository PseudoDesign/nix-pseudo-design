Delivery model

Investigate → Deliver → Hand off

  1. 01

    Investigate

    Trace the real problem across layers. Test assumptions, narrow uncertainty, and find the leverage point.

  2. 02

    Deliver

    Turn the useful answer into working software, infrastructure, tooling, or a repaired workflow.

  3. 03

    Hand off

    Leave behind an operable system, clear decisions, and enough context for the next change to be straightforward.

Working principle

Inherited systems are welcome. Frozen assumptions are not.

Legacy technology can be understood, stabilized, and improved. Progress starts with permission to change what the investigation proves should change.
