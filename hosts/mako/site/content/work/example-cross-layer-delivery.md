## Problem

Replace this guidance with the observable problem and why it mattered. Describe the system behavior, not the presumed cause.

## Constraints

Record the technical, organizational, operational, and delivery constraints that shaped the work. Separate real constraints from assumptions that could be changed.

## Investigation

Explain how the problem was traced across layers, what evidence changed the direction of the work, and which plausible paths were ruled out.

## Delivered System

Describe the concrete deliverables: software, infrastructure, tooling, documentation, or workflow changes. Make the boundary of the engagement clear.

## Demonstrated Result

Show what the delivered system proved in operation. Prefer measured behavior, reproducible checks, or a concrete before-and-after comparison.

## Handoff

Document what the receiving team can operate independently, where the important decisions live, and what remains intentionally open for future work.
